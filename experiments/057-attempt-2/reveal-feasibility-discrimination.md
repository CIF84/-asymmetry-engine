## 14. F1 finding

**Finalist; provisional controllability leader.** A fixed-scope paid brief can use demonstrated decision compression without building a service platform. [Stripe's hosted collection](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links) and [Upwork's priced research catalog](https://www.upwork.com/services/market-research) establish plausible exchange routes. Neither supplies AE's buyer, credibility, delivery capacity or profitable acquisition.

The repeat-output class could reduce marginal work, but generic alerting is already served in examples such as [TED](https://ted.europa.eu/en/help/about-search-results). A paid variation must resolve something the exact free alternative leaves unresolved for a particular buyer. “AI-generated research” is not a differentiated claim. Initial manual work is acceptable only if its input/output contract can stabilize; unlimited bespoke analysis would earn service evidence while failing the mature burden objective.

The decisive missing facts are a qualified reachable decision owner, an honest deliverable/price, conversion and measured delivery/exception minutes. These are accessible to a bounded sale experiment, but have not been obtained here. F1 captures a payment; causal benefit to the buyer remains a separate measurement.

## 15. F2 finding

A small original model could be licensed before a full product exists. [Highcharts' OEM arrangement](https://shop.highcharts.com/?accordion=accordion-region-1-0) is a concrete example of a commercial embedded-rights topology; it says nothing about AE's ability to sell one.

## 18. F5 finding

**Explicitly considered, researched and retained conditionally.** This can be the shortest path to attributable net gain if the operator already controls a real activity with reducible cash costs. Two valid candidate operations would be an existing recurring compute workload with bills and service-quality requirements, or an existing software-seat/procurement process with invoices, renewal dates and cancellation authority. Required control includes permission to inspect the baseline and separately approve any consequential change; possession of tools is insufficient.

The human's durable input leaves access UNKNOWN. No baseline, spend amount, cash gain or controlled workload is invented. [AWS's native recommendations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/cost-optimization-hub.html) are an exact-alternative challenge, and [Microsoft's reduction windows](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/commerce/licenses/buy-licenses?view=o365-worldwide) show why technically unassigning a seat is not immediately retained cash.

A valid F5 event needs comparable useful output, actual reduced liability/payment, and all migration, quality, outage, switching and monitoring costs. Normalize for workload and price changes; do not claim a reduction caused by a smaller business as an optimization effect. Time saved without a measured cash consequence is time evidence. Cancelling hypothetical future spending is not established captured value. Repeated bills after one change can confirm persistence but are not independent discoveries.

Savings are bounded by the controlled operation's economic base. Serving outside owners for a fee shifts AE's capture to F1/F4, licensing the method to F2, and buying operations adds F6. That compositional boundary prevents F5 from appearing to offer unlimited customer-free factory replication. A documented operation with a material, reversible, quality-preserving gain would put F5 ahead of the present pair for first proof; novelty alone does not.
