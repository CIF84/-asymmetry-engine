## 10. Candidates deepened

Exactly three candidates were deepened.

### D1 / C08 — Jira automation allowance

- **Strongest support:** a current administrator disclosed a numerical allowance and a concrete enable-now versus wait decision.
- **Exact resolution:** [Atlassian automation licensing](https://www.atlassian.com/licensing/automation), [expanded usage resources](https://www.atlassian.com/resources/usage), the [pricing transition announcement](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/company-news/expanded-usage-based-pricing), and accepted native answers explain the transition and available guardrail.
- **Residual gap:** none material to the posted decision.
- **Disposition:** **KILL — EXACT RESOLUTION**. Another memo would duplicate current authoritative guidance.

### D2 / C11 — PAX ERP “without AI” positioning

- **Strongest support:** the founder directly exposed a product-positioning decision with product and acquisition consequences.
- **Exact status check:** current [PAX ERP documentation](https://www.paxerp.com/docs) contains a “Paxy AI” section, while the [current product page](https://www.paxerp.com/erp) describes the live offer.
- **Interpretation limit:** current product state shows only that the original binary is no longer safely open. It does not establish why it changed or that the discussion caused it.
- **Disposition:** **KILL — DECISION CLOSED**.

### D3 / C10 — Production `gpt-realtime-2.1` migration

- **Strongest support:** a production-system owner reports a fixed qualification corpus, repeatable tool-use regressions, a named deprecated source snapshot, and a January 2027 migration constraint. The actor controls its own migration decision and the consequence is operationally material.
- **Exact-resolution check:** official [`gpt-realtime-2.1` documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-realtime-2.1) establishes a reasoning model with configurable reasoning effort, instruction following, and function calling. Official [`gpt-realtime` documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-realtime) identifies the earlier snapshot as deprecated. Neither source resolves equivalence for the actor’s private workload or promises an extension, deterministic replacement, or early-access path.
- **Residual gap:** the public record does not show the paired per-intent distribution needed to tell whether the bounded next resolution is configuration/prompt/tool-contract adaptation or a vendor-roadmap dependency.
- **Cheapest decisive discriminator:** one anonymized paired qualification matrix from the actor’s already-existing fixed corpus, comparing the legacy snapshot and `gpt-realtime-2.1` under the same tool schemas, prompts, and declared reasoning setting. It must report per-intent pass/fail, required-tool-call success, wrong/no-tool rate, latency where already captured, and the actor’s pre-existing pass threshold. This is one evidence package, not a request for a new broad test program.
- **Stop result:** if the matrix localizes failures to a bounded configuration, prompt, or tool-contract class, a FORGE brief may be constructible; if failures remain broad or only a vendor capability/roadmap change can resolve them, kill on recoverability/resolution value.
- **Disposition:** **HOLD — ONE BOUNDED DISCRIMINATOR**. No contact or request was made.

Exact resolution was dominant in one terminal disposition and materially decisive in two closed-decision dispositions. It remained valuable but was not the plurality terminal class in this small set.

No other candidate retained both a live decision and a non-duplicate, bounded resolution gap.

## 23. Comparison with Experiments 031 and 032

- **Actor access:** as in Experiment 032, actor-first surfaces produced several direct operators; nevertheless, authority failed early in four candidates because a visible account or authoritative channel was not equivalent to authority over the target decision.
- **Exact resolution:** it remained a cheap discriminator, consistent with both earlier runs, but was not the only bottleneck. Native status checks closed three candidates before deeper work.
- **Interaction observability:** the aligned ledger reshaped C10 without requiring every state. It separated delivery, exposure, authoritative-account engagement, decision-state change, downstream action, and economic effect before any intervention was contemplated.
- **Candidate specificity:** candidates were decision-specific, but broad consultations and procurement notices still generated weak actor topology and were killed quickly.
- **Burden:** the ledger added little burden because it was applied only to C10 after earlier fatal gates passed. No matched timing denominator supports a speed claim against Experiments 031 or 032.

## 24. Fatal-gate / dominant-bottleneck findings

The plurality terminal failure was **ACTOR / AUTHORITY** (four of twelve). Three decisions were already closed, two lacked bounded uncertainty, one failed recoverability, and one had an exact resolver. The sole HOLD shows the downstream bottleneck after those gates: private but naturally bounded actor-held evidence needed to determine whether a residual resolution is constructible. The aligned policy did not force ledger work onto dead candidates.
