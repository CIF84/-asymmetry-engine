# Experiment 055 — V2.1 visible-copy review

## Coverage and method

[The browser inventory](visible-copy-inventory.json) records original V2 and V2.1 text, region, element and accessible/title/option wording. It covers the initial surface, all 33 aggregate explanations, all six historical details and all four demonstration scenarios; expanded precision is separately recorded for each historical detail. Collapsed detail content is excluded from visible primary text. Exact title attributes and original wording are also inventoried; those are precision on demand, not replacements for the human-facing label.

Every primary-interface string source was reviewed: static HTML; generated Engine labels, legend, lifecycle/blocker/run counts; audit titles, results, membership/empty states; control groups/cards; possibility rows; record selector; detail identity/roles/dimensions/knowledge/trajectory/frontier/source explanations; made-up scenarios; navigation, buttons, accessibility labels and load-failure copy. Saved record names, experiment IDs, dates, source paths and numerical values remain identifiable. The inventory uses a deduplicated entry dictionary and per-state indexes. It is evidence of coverage, not a measure of readability improvement. More or fewer strings does not establish comprehension.

## Complete copy decisions

- **Overview:** changed machine nouns into questions and short explanations. Kept visible 0/6/3/3 counts, different dates, sample limits, overlap, zero-record versus zero-opportunity distinction, and unestablished downstream outcomes. No health score, conversion rate or claim of a current census.
- **Distributions:** renamed presentation labels only. Paused is conditional, ended is scoped to the tested idea, and needs reassessment retains REVIEW. Main-blocker counts describe records rather than every cause. No barrier is claimed cleared.
- **Audit:** plain result/unit explanation and records first; unchanged exact result, denominator, population, horizon, derivation, missingness and source links in expandable detail. All six historical admissions and current external states remain UNKNOWN. Run candidates remain separate and heterogeneous.
- **Human action:** plain historical reason and condition first; original permission, requested action and inaction meanings remain available. No historical instruction becomes a task today. Each made-up scenario has an explicit boundary and retains its exact instruction on demand.
- **Opportunities/evidence:** each original record has hand-written idea, identity, six dimension summaries, knowns, unknowns, unproven claims, barrier, possible next check, roles and timeline summaries. Exact original statements remain expandable. No universal substring replacement is used to generate these summaries.
- **Precision on demand:** exact terms are in counting help, titled state labels, original control details, original hypothesis/verdict, identity/blocker details, dimension evidence, knowledge statements, timeline events and verbatim 054 records. Missing evidence is described as not known, never as failure or absence.
- **Layout:** retained the four distinct sections and all drill-down routes; allowed longer questions to wrap and increased small text. No added workflow, capture, monitoring, ranking or action feature.
- **Acceptance:** new separate packet uses equivalent plain questions in the same order, preserves original question references and separate timing, discloses prior V2 exposure, and requires attempt 2 to be recorded separately. No human response or acceptance judgment is invented.

## Quality-check outcome

Mechanical coverage and semantic comparison pass. The copy aims to be understandable to a business operator without the research history and explains why each field matters. This is an implementation judgment, not proof that the human-language confounder is removed. Human comprehension and lower translation burden remain untested until attempt 2.
