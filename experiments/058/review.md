## 4. G3 review — execution versus strategy
**EXECUTION PASS; STRATEGIC GATE DEFECT IDENTIFIED.**

The executor applied G3 as written and often carefully stated that free/cheap alternatives do not automatically eliminate paid convenience or specialization.

But the gate still asks the wrong Phase-II question.

G3 currently requires:
> existing free/cheap exact solutions do not already make AE's proposed bounded resolution redundant.

That is appropriate when AE is testing whether a novel **information asymmetry** exists.

It is not sufficient as a fatal gate when AE is testing whether it can enter an existing **economic flow**.

Commercial opportunity can exist despite an adequate incumbent solution when there is a viable wedge in:
- audience/segment;
- distribution;
- trust;
- convenience;
- localization;
- workflow/integration;
- timeliness;
- packaging;
- price/cost structure;
- acquisition economics;
- monetization route.

The relevant Phase-II question is closer to:
> **Is there a specific economically testable wedge that gives AE a plausible reason to win some transactions despite existing solutions?**

An incumbent can therefore be positive evidence:
> **solution exists + transactions occur + capture mechanism works**

rather than merely a kill signal.

This review does **not** assert that C01/C04/C11 are good opportunities. It establishes that G3 killed them before asking the Phase-II commercial-wedge question.

## 8. Search-depth review
The search was intentionally shallow: research stopped after 8m10s and the full session after 13m57s.

That is acceptable for obvious fatal contradictions, but it is **not enough to establish absence of commercial wedges** across 12 markets.

This matters because commercial-wedge discovery generally requires one level deeper than “does a competent alternative exist?”

The correct response is not to retroactively invalidate the run or extend it. It is to change the next search unit/method:
- start from proven monetized flows;
- identify one concrete segment/distribution/decision wedge;
- spend enough bounded effort to test that wedge;
- stop quickly if none is visible.

Do not interpret “12 candidates in 14 minutes” as demonstrated high-quality commercial opportunity coverage.

## 10. What should replace G3
Candidate for the next contract:

### Commercial Wedge Gate
A candidate passes only if there is a **specific, observable reason AE could capture some economic flow despite incumbents**, supported enough to justify a cheap market treatment.

Possible wedge classes may include:
- underserved audience/segment;
- superior or cheaper distribution access;
- materially better decision compression/convenience;
- localization;
- faster/fresher resolution;
- cross-provider synthesis incumbents do not supply;
- workflow/integration advantage;
- monetization/pricing structure;
- another explicit mechanism.

The gate should require a **named wedge hypothesis**, not proof that the wedge works.

Existing competent paid/free solutions become:
- proof of market/transaction topology;
- competitors/benchmarks;
- exact-alternative challenges to the **wedge**, not automatic killers of the underlying market.

If no specific wedge is visible cheaply, reject.
