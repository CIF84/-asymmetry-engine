## Contradictions and Ambiguities

- Experiment 031 says 13 of 14 candidates were killed before deepening while also recording 3 deepened candidates. Aggregate counts are retained; no shallow-kill metric is inferred.
- Experiment 030 labels initialization `DELIVERY UNKNOWN` despite verified public publication. Read narrowly, surface delivery is verified while delivery to or visibility by the fixed actor is UNKNOWN.
- Experiment 032 reports “0 full; 1 with one bounded uncertainty.” The latter is a bounded survivor, not a full survivor or completed FORGE handoff.
- Experiment 035’s ~18-minute preparation/publication measure and ~1-minute publication/verification measure may overlap.
- Approximate, “roughly,” and “under” durations are ESTIMATED. Targets, ceilings, calendar intervals, observation windows, and commit timestamps are not active time.
- Zero external spend excludes unavailable model/compute cost, which remains UNKNOWN.
- Experiment 020’s checkpoint explicitly classifies the run HIGH YIELD despite the later cross-check saying no explicit yield field existed.

## Supported Conclusions

- These experiments repeatedly produced decision-relevant learning at recorded external spend of €0, except Experiment 020’s recorded US$0.09.
- Cheap rejection is supported within several bounded RADAR runs: 020 parked one candidate, 021 killed 11, 031 killed 14, and 032 killed 9 while retaining one bounded survivor.
- The evidence shows qualitative bottleneck movement: exact-resolution competition → candidate generation/distribution → actor-visible topology → dependency sequencing → disposable resolution → verified publication.
- Safeguards prevented invalid inference and unauthorized action: 026 stopped without outreach; 030 and 035 required explicit authorization; 031–034 performed no actor interaction.
- Later artifacts preserve increasingly explicit operational telemetry, but only 031–032 record exact run-level active minutes and zero human interventions.
- Experiments 030 and 035 establish authorization-bounded public publication and verified surface delivery only.

## Unsupported Conclusions

- No defensible quantitative claim that the Engine is becoming faster over time.
- No valid aggregate funnel, conversion rate, or efficiency ratio across RADAR, FORGE, acquisition, publication, and observation phases.
- No historical compute-cost trend, total-cost trend, ROI, hourly value, or cost multiplier.
- No proof that zero external spend means zero total cost.
- No proof that publication caused actor exposure, comprehension, trust, decision change, action, economic value, revenue, repeatability, or market demand.
- No proof that one decision-ready artifact or one delivered interaction generalizes.
- No proof that zero observed initialization responses is a negative behavioral result.
- No human-attention efficiency claim: consistent human active minutes are unavailable.

## Confidence and Limitations

Confidence is HIGH for quoted experiment-level counts, explicit spend, safeguards, verdicts, and initialization states; MEDIUM for reconstructed qualitative operating-policy conclusions; LOW for any cross-experiment efficiency trend.

Limitations include heterogeneous phases and units, mostly estimated historical time, run-level rather than per-candidate timing, inconsistent human-safeguard schemas, unknown compute cost, right-censored 030/035 observation, the 031 count ambiguity, and a small non-comparable sample. UNKNOWN values were not converted to zero.
