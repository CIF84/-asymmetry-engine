## 7. Fixture/representation format

The structured fixture is `experiments/051/opportunities.js`, a plain versioned JavaScript object containing six Opportunity records. Plain JavaScript was chosen over fetched JSON so the viewer opens directly from `file://` without a server or browser fetch restrictions. Each record contains:

- stable opportunity ID and historical experiment range;
- evidence horizon;
- preserved historical disposition;
- separately derived lifecycle state and basis;
- thesis, significance, actor, beneficiary, and buyer/payer;
- six independent dimension objects with state, evidence class, summary, blocker, and next observation where supported;
- dominant blocker, next discriminator, and reactivation condition where contingent;
- knowns, unknowns, and explicitly unproven claims;
- immutable experiment-history entries and repository source paths.

The file is static evidence, not a canonical Opportunity model or persistence mechanism.

## 8. Opportunity lifecycle vocabulary result

The default vocabulary was usable without expansion:

- `ACTIVE`: 0 fixtures;
- `DORMANT`: 4 fixtures — cocoa, Canadian tariff, CRM, realtime migration;
- `TERMINAL`: 1 fixture — EV smart-charging;
- `REVIEW`: 1 fixture — Superset.

Zero `ACTIVE` fixtures is an evidence result, not a UI defect. Repository-only historical horizons do not justify claiming that an external decision is currently live. `DORMANT` is used only for a surviving thesis with a stated contingent reactivation condition. `TERMINAL` is reserved for the EV residual-resolution thesis invalidated by exact competition. `REVIEW` preserves Superset's established historical decision effect while refusing to infer current project or commercial state.

## 9. Six-dimensional vocabulary result

The categorical dimension vocabulary `NEAR / MEDIUM / FAR / BLOCKED / UNKNOWN` was sufficient. No numeric values, weighted totals, composite distance, rank, priority, or auto-recommendation were added. Each category is accompanied by prose evidence and an evidence class. Regulatory feasibility additionally uses the specification's independent `CLEAR / BOUNDED / REVIEW REQUIRED / BLOCKED / ILLEGAL / UNKNOWN` vocabulary only where supported; no fixture is labeled `ILLEGAL`.

## 10. Resolution-distance findings

- Canadian, CRM, and Superset are `NEAR` because decision-ready resolutions were recorded; Superset also survived direct actor challenge and refinement.
- Cocoa is `MEDIUM`: a concrete proposition and pilot were specified, but actor validation never occurred.
- Realtime is `BLOCKED`: the actor-held paired matrix required to construct a bounded resolution was not obtained.
- EV is `BLOCKED` at the opportunity level because an adequate existing resolver falsified the residual-gap premise; its `TERMINAL` lifecycle distinguishes this structural result from a contingent block.

## 11. Access-distance findings

Access is the dominant constraint for cocoa and Canadian tariff and a material constraint for realtime. CRM demonstrates why public surface access and verified publication do not establish actor exposure. Superset is the sole `NEAR` access case because the fixed actor engaged the exact resolution on the same decision surface. The representation therefore keeps surface access, delivery, and exposure conceptually separate in the evidence text rather than compressing them into one favorable state.

## 12. Adoption-distance findings

Adoption remains `UNKNOWN` for cocoa, EV, Canadian, CRM, and realtime because no valid downstream use was observed. Superset is `MEDIUM`: the actor stated a concrete ship-now disposition and contract refinement, but formal adoption, code change, merge, shipping, and end-user effect were not observed. Stated next action is not silently converted into downstream action.

## 13. Control-distance findings

Control is `BLOCKED` where legitimate execution capability, authorization-compatible reach, or delivery observability failed (cocoa, Canadian, realtime). CRM is `MEDIUM/BOUNDED`: one authorized interaction executed cleanly, but its effect path was measurement-limited and the historical interaction is closed. Superset is `NEAR/BOUNDED`: one authorized comment plus one final read-only observation produced interpretable evidence. EV remains `UNKNOWN` because exact-resolution competition terminated the branch before an actor-facing control test was warranted.

## 14. Regulatory-distance findings

Canadian tariff is `MEDIUM` with `REVIEW REQUIRED` because origin, remission, and exception facts can remain case-specific even when the schedule is authoritative. Every other fixture remains `UNKNOWN`; no absence of regulatory research is rendered as `CLEAR`, and regulation is never treated as a negative score. No new legal research or illegality inference was performed.

## 15. Economic-distance findings

Cocoa is `MEDIUM` because it uniquely contains an explicit direct paid-pilot audience, offer, price, and transaction threshold, while still lacking valid exposure, WTP, or payment. Canadian, CRM, Superset, realtime, and EV are `FAR` for distinct evidence reasons. Superset demonstrates the key separation most clearly: material actor decision-state effect is known, yet buyer/payer, economic effect, WTP, transaction, repeatability, and value capture remain unestablished. `FAR` is never used as a numeric penalty or automatic ranking input.
