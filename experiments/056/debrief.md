Search space and experimental regime are independent axes.

The original nine-regime tournament was useful within predominantly customer-facing commercialization, but it mixed those axes. It omitted T3 direct exploitation and did not explicitly compare T4 attention or T6 licensing as top-level families. Selecting a preferred substrate from that incomplete space was therefore stronger than the evidence supported. The independent review upholds the human objection about conversational anchoring and the direct-exploitation omission.
