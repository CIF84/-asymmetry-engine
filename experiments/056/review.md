## 1. Contamination-control method
The independent reviewer first reconstructed AE's plausible value-capture topology from repository evidence through Experiment 055, deliberately excluding the Experiment 056 conclusions. Only after freezing that map was the 056 tournament/result read.

This was necessary because affiliate/referral entered the preceding human conversation as an illustrative idea and was then explicitly named in SPEC-056. The question is not whether affiliate is attractive; it is whether it would survive an independently complete strategy search.

## 2. Blind topology result
The blind reconstruction found at least six materially different capture families:

1. **Sell the resolution** — paid information, service, API/tool/SaaS.
2. **Monetize a third-party decision/transaction** — affiliate/referral, lead generation, brokerage-like fees.
3. **Directly exploit the asymmetry** — trading, price/marketplace arbitrage, prediction/forecast positions, or other lawful own-account economic actions.
4. **Monetize attention** — ads/sponsorship/platform revenue.
5. **Own the product/transaction** — digital product, SaaS, marketplace/commerce.
6. **License/sell the mechanism or asset** — incumbent/complementary-asset commercialization.

Search strategy and experimental regime are orthogonal to these capture topologies.

## 3. Affiliate contamination result
Would affiliate/referral emerge near the top if the human had never suggested it?

**YES as a strong topology family; NO as an independently justified preferred implementation.**

Its structural appeal follows independently from AE:
- existing transaction streams;
- low owned fulfillment;
- decision-resolution fit;
- potentially observable click/conversion/commission events.

But pre-056 repository evidence does not establish:
- qualifying affiliate programs;
- acquisition economics;
- attribution reliability;
- commission/margin adequacy;
- maintenance/freshness cost;
- an AE-specific distribution advantage.

Therefore “affiliate/referral first to qualify” is stronger than the evidence currently supports.

## 4. Direct-exploitation omission
The 056 nine-regime tournament does not include a strategy in which AE captures value by acting on the asymmetry itself.

This is a material omission, not a minor missing example.

Direct exploitation changes the economic topology:
ASYMMETRY → RESOLUTION → OWN ECONOMIC ACTION → P&L

Potential advantages:
- removes buyer/WTP/customer-access layers;
- monetization is intrinsic;
- repeated market observations can support recurrence;
- automation can be native;
- economic telemetry can be comparatively direct.

Potential disadvantages:
- capital at risk;
- statistical false-discovery/overfitting burden;
- fees/slippage/latency;
- sophisticated competition and edge decay;
- potentially larger regulatory/control distance;
- monitoring/risk-management burden;
- one profitable action may be weak causal evidence.

Repository evidence through 055 does not establish a direct-exploitation edge. The point is that the topology must be considered before selecting Phase II's substrate, not that trading should replace affiliate.
