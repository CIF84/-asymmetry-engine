## 8. Research overhang and sunk cost

The [strategic checkpoint](../../docs/STRATEGIC_CHECKPOINT_001_029.md) already warns against an entrepreneurial immune system that rejects everything. **DERIVED:** A new dashboard, ontology, architecture review or generic experiment-policy improvement has a high burden of justification while no valid capture test exists. 051–055 earned bounded usability and accounting; they did not earn an ongoing representation program. Past construction costs are sunk. Keep evidence and tested mechanics, but suspend obligations to exercise every module or finish an imagined full Engine.

Do not discard rigor to look entrepreneurial. Preserve exact-resolution checks, authority, provenance, truthful promises and exposure accounting; compress their execution around the live offer. Documentation and review remain necessary but should be proportional and bundled into each treatment rather than counted as separate economic progress.

## 10. FORGE assessment and MTR candidate

**RECORDED:** 025 produced a validated twelve-line brief in approximately 30 minutes; 033–034 used approximately 43 minutes combined for a bounded dependency/resolution chain. **PROPOSED:** Extend FORGE's deliverable from decision-ready to **Minimum Transactable Resolution (MTR)** where a valid market treatment is feasible. An MTR is a bounded resolution plus a real exchange/referral route, credible exposure, fulfillment plan and observable economic event. A landing page alone is not an MTR. A sham checkout, unfulfillable promise or unapproved affiliate link is not a valid shortcut.

Before launch, its contract must answer:

## 11. E versus M: when pre-validation stops

| Regime | Best use | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| E — evidence before build | Cheap decisive exact-resolution check; missing critical input; material safety/control question; expensive or irreversible delivery | Repeated proxies never test whether anyone will exchange value |
| M — monetization as experiment | A truthful deliverable/referral is cheap, reversible, reachable and measurable; payment can reveal more than another demand proxy | Cheap building conceals expensive exposure, support or obligations; low traffic is mistaken for rejection |

**PROPOSED crossover:** After the fatal screen, compare the *whole* cost of the next useful proxy with the smallest valid market treatment: construction + acquisition + fulfillment + support + controls + human time + waiting. Choose M when it answers the binding commercial question more directly at an acceptable bounded cost. Retain E when one cheap fact can kill the treatment or when a critical unknown makes selling/referring invalid. “AI makes coding cheap” is a hypothesis, not repository evidence of declining total launch cost.

Allow at most **one candidate-specific pre-market discriminator after admission**, inside a two-experiment admission-to-exposure limit. A second proxy needs a concrete explanation of why no truthful MTR exists within the cap; the default is park, not an unlimited validation chain. Price response, offer comprehension, channel conversion and nonfatal residual-value uncertainty can be tested in the market. Missing authority, untrustworthy core claims and disproportionate downside cannot.

Experimentation is still the learning mechanism. Its selection and decomposition can become a bottleneck when each upstream refinement earns another packet while exchange stays optional. That is the suspected allocation failure; 049 does **not** demonstrate that mandatory serialization or human approval delay caused the commercial gap. Bundle where useful and measure delays prospectively instead of removing governance on an unsupported efficiency claim.

**Relation to 050:** Its rejection of indiscriminate revenue-first P1 stands. Do not add a price ask to closed CRM/Superset/realtime interactions. The reset changes the selection topology for *new* opportunities and permits value and capture to be tested together where justified. It does not claim an earlier valid payment test was historically available and skipped. ROADMAP's value-before-capture wording would need a separately approved bounded alignment if this recommendation is adopted; no living rule changes here.

## 13. Commercial observability

Prefer the joint topology: visible live decision → identifiable buyer/counterparty → existing economic activity → normal exchange/referral route → measurable qualified exposure → observable economic event → bounded fulfillment. A public complaint, an actor account and an existing checkout each establish only part of that chain.

Preserve four separate denominators: eligible audience reached; qualified people exposed to this offer/resolution; downstream action such as referral or checkout; accepted/settled economic events. Record exclusions, deduplication limits, bots/internal tests, source and horizon. A page view is qualified exposure only if the preregistered qualification evidence supports that interpretation. Repeated sessions are not automatically different buyers. Anonymous users do not become supported 054 actor-decision instances.

**No sales with verified qualified exposure is different evidence from no sales with unknown exposure.** The former can weaken a bounded offer/channel proposition at adequate sample size; the latter primarily diagnoses access or measurement. Merchant attribution gaps and pending settlement are censoring, not zero commissions. Evidence precision must not exceed platform visibility.

## 16. Affiliate/referral assessment

**Classification: PREFERRED first substrate to qualify, conditionally; commercial viability UNKNOWN.** Its structural fit is strongest when AE improves a purchase decision inside an already functioning transaction stream, earns an attributable fee and leaves merchant payment/product fulfillment substantially with the merchant. This reduces what AE must build before economic evidence, if those arrangements actually exist for the chosen case.

That preference fails if a permitted channel cannot produce qualified exposure within the test budget, if conversion/commission cannot be observed in time, if realistic margin cannot fund acquisition/maintenance, or if no useful decision advantage remains over exact alternatives. SEO-only distribution with an unknown ramp does not satisfy a short market-truth test. Paid acquisition is not presumed affordable. An unpaid outbound click is not WTP for AE or captured value.

Before any later execution, verify program eligibility/terms, traffic restrictions, disclosure requirements, commission basis, reversals, attribution window and available reports for the exact offer. These are unknown dependencies here, not current legal or program advice. Merchant/program concentration, ranking/search changes, content staleness, tracking loss and competitive responses belong in the maintenance and downside ledger. Commercial incentives must not silently change the resolution recommendation.

If affiliate fails the admission screen, compare **one bounded paid information/one-purpose tool offer** against the same buyer/exposure and low-maintenance constraints. Do not spend a tranche constructing a referral engine to defend the initial preference.

## 17. Content/factory assessment

The possible reusable mechanism is: targeted transaction/decision observation → evidence-backed decision model → small resolution artifact → qualified distribution → attributed exchange → observed failure/success → better next treatment. The first implementation can be manual. There is no entitlement to mass content, a CMS, automated publishing or generic content generation.

Separate **opportunity economics** (one audience/offer/cohort's cash, delivery and support) from **factory economics** (time and cost to qualify and launch another comparable offer; shared acquisition, sourcing, validation and maintenance). Count shared costs once and disclose allocation; free reuse is not assumed. A modest paid result that permits a cheaper second launch can be more informative than a larger bespoke consulting payment, but hypothetical reuse cannot outweigh consistently negative contribution.

What might compound: known audience access, conversion diagnosis, reusable decision fields, tested source checks, calibrated claims, merchant/payment integration and a repeatable artifact process. What does not yet compound: a growing number of pages or internal records. A second comparable launch must measure actual reuse and maintenance before calling this a factory. Content can be the treatment; it must not become a production-volume objective.

## 18. Proven-market-adaptation challenge

**Preferred over novel/residual discovery for this tranche.** Existing transaction → underserved variation → adapted exchange topology asks AE to contribute decision quality where economic behavior already occurs. It lowers the number of simultaneous hypotheses: AE need not invent both a buyer's reason to spend and a mechanism to collect value.

The adversarial case against it is serious: established markets can be fully served, heavily contested or inaccessible. “Proven market” proves neither this variant's gap nor AE's right to reproduce another business's material. Learn from topology using legitimate evidence; do not assume copying content, brands, datasets or proprietary operations is permissible.

The exact-resolution gate becomes more precise, not weaker: an adequate freely/cheaply available resolver for the *same audience, inputs, job and decision moment* defeats the resolution-advantage thesis unless an independently evidenced access/distribution advantage defines a different bounded proposition. Competition is not itself fatal; fabricated differentiation or feature expansion is not a rescue. EV 020 stays closed. No old opportunity is reopened by this distinction.

## 21. Time to Market Truth

**Adopt provisionally as a Phase II operating metric.** Record calendar elapsed and agent/human active time separately from explicit prospective admission to the first *valid revealed-preference event*, or to a preregistered negative result under credible qualified exposure. Also record time to first qualified exposure and time to settled capture. Never reset clocks when renaming an offer or changing a channel for the same admitted hypothesis. Waiting/authorization delays remain visible with reasons; no-event outcomes are right-censored.

| Substrate | Useful intermediate behavior | Qualifying economic market truth | Capture and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affiliate/referral | Qualified offer view, outbound click | Attributed eligible purchase/action accepted as commission-bearing under the actual agreement | Settled commission separately; pending/reversed attribution not cash; no claim buyer paid AE |
| Lead-gen | Relevant submission with permission for its intended use | Actual lead buyer accepts a qualified lead under binding payable terms, or pays | Accepted payable lead is exchange evidence; settled receipt is capture; end sale and beneficiary effect may remain UNKNOWN |
| Paid information | Sample read, relevant inquiry | Real paid order/commitment to a specified deliverable with credible fulfillment | Receipt and delivery/refund status separate; interest survey is insufficient |
| Direct payment / micro-tool | Qualified checkout initiation | Real purchase for a deliverable with clear terms and capacity | Fulfilled, non-refunded receipt is stronger; test/self payments excluded |
| SaaS | Activation and meaningful use | Paid use/contract commitment by a real customer | Renewal/repeated paid use needed for recurrence; free signup is not exchange |

Positive behavior at an intermediate layer is useful diagnostic evidence without satisfying the economic endpoint. Payment does not prove causal economic benefit to the user, optimal choice, profit or recurrence. Those assertions remain separately testable.

A genuine qualifying purchase can establish that purchaser's exposure and positive exchange even if the full audience denominator is unknown; record the bounded event without inventing a conversion rate. Negative demand inference needs the stronger denominator. Neither rule requires verified human-only authorship when an accountable authorized counterparty transacts; authority and authorship remain separate under the existing operating model.

For a negative test, preregister the exact offer/cohort, qualified exposure definition, credible event capture, minimum economically relevant response and observation/attribution window. Use costs and net value to choose the floor, not an arbitrary universal traffic quota. As a planning calculation only, if independent comparable qualified exposures and near-complete event observation were defensible, zero events in `N` exposures has probability `(1-p_min)^N` at the chosen minimum rate. A 5% tail criterion needs `N >= log(0.05)/log(1-p_min)` (59 at a hypothetical 5% floor; 299 at 1%). These are design examples, not measured conversion priors or mandatory significance tests. Clustered/repeat traffic, unknown qualification or attribution break the calculation. If a credible denominator cannot be bought or reached within the cap, report access/measurement failure; do not lower the threshold after seeing zero sales.

## 32. Recurrence and factory rule

At admission, name what repeats: comparable buyers, purchase decisions, paid updates, referrals or paid tool use. Also name what must be refreshed and who bears support. An event-specific sale with no plausible recurrence is a weak fit even if it is easy money.

After a first valid economic event, favor **one replication in an independently qualified cohort or unrelated customer decision**, using the same resolution/acquisition mechanism. Record incremental launch effort and all manual work. Different page text alone is not independent replication; repeated sessions or friendly/internal purchases do not supply repeat demand.

Before deeper investment, seek at least two fulfilled/accepted paid outcomes from unrelated counterparties or independently qualified cohorts across two observation windows, with credible contribution after acquisition/delivery costs and a plausible operator-time path. This is an early continuation gate, not statistical proof of market size, durable recurrence or passive income. Long-run recurrence still requires later observation.

Automation is earned only after paid use plus at least two observed repetitions expose the same stable mechanical bottleneck, and a bounded automation change can plausibly pay back in the remaining planned repetitions. Otherwise reuse a manual checklist or artifact. Never automate a negative-margin offer to make it appear scalable.
