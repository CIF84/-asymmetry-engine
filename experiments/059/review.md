## 8. C04 P7 comparable exposure
**NOT ESTABLISHED AT Q DEPTH.**

The designated r/podcasting weekly services thread appears to permit disclosed service offers. That establishes **permission surface**, not **qualified-audience plausibility**.

The treatment requires a highly specific denominator:
- adult-course business owner;
- existing licensed course;
- 100–500 paying learners;
- up to three feeds;
- purchase decision open;
- launch within 30 days.

The report then targets **30 confirmed qualified exposures in 28 days** from four weekly service comments.

No evidence in the report supports the proposition that this surface contains enough of that population to make 30 confirmed exposures a plausible bounded target.

This matters because a likely-zero-density channel would turn the next experiment into another access failure rather than an economic test.

The review does not require an existing audience or guaranteed reach. It requires a credible route to the denominator.
