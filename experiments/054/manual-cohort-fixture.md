## Manual contract and reading rules

Each row carries these field groups:

1. **Referent/identity:** existing local referent, family thesis, supported instance label or UNKNOWN, owner, decision object and horizon, identity status and relationship.
2. **Lineage:** originating evidence, original candidate label where recorded, ordered experiments and their preserved dispositions.
3. **Admission:** historical admission event/time, bounded cohort inclusion and rationale, with separate horizons.
4. **Frontier:** independent evidence assertions, not a highest-stage integer.
5. **Lifecycle:** state, family/instance scope, effective evidence horizon, reason and evidence class.
6. **Control:** historical action/authorization boundary and waiting/blocking condition; current control evidence separately.
7. **Blocker/reactivation:** contingent/structural/unresolved interpretation, conditions, freshness checks and identity route.
8. **Roles/economics:** actor, beneficiary, buyer, payer, consequence and exchange path, without role conflation.
9. **Provenance/missingness:** sources, classes, horizons and material ambiguities attached to the preceding assertions.

**R = RECORDED**, explicitly preserved in repository evidence, including an actor's claim or a historical verdict. It does not mean independently verified current truth. **D = DERIVED**, a semantic interpretation/grouping/calculation. **E = ESTIMATED**, an explicitly approximate source quantity. **U = UNKNOWN**, absent, insufficient, untested or unobservable; never zero. Sources at each row apply to the cited assertion. Row horizon is the default only where no earlier observation horizon is stated. Historical specifications establish intention, not execution.

For frontier cells: **R-supported** = documented evidence exists; **R-refuted** = a bounded premise was tested and rejected; **R-not reached** = the record says the step did not validly execute/produce the relevant result; **U** = no supported conclusion. R-not reached does not assert absence of private real-world effects. Downstream effect U remains U even if no experiment tested it. Delivery means delivery of AE's treatment to its fixed surface, not existence of an external resolver.

A family is a bounded reusable job/resolution thesis; a decision instance needs an attributable owner, a specific decision object and an event/horizon distinguishing it from another decision. An actor class, sample configuration, test manifest or batch is insufficient. A URL is a provenance pointer, not the sole identity key. Unknown identity stays provisional and is excluded from supported-instance counts; new signals and experiments do not automatically add families or instances. Same-instance evidence requires compatible owner/object/horizon; conflicting or insufficient evidence preserves UNRESOLVED rather than merging or splitting silently.

The retrospective 054 inclusion event is **R: SPEC-054 selects these six chains**, horizon 2026-10-07. Historical inventory admission and its timestamp are **U for all six**. Recorded discovery/advancement/PARK/HOLD events remain lineage; none are relabeled historical inventory admission. Prospective manual capture would explicitly record the inclusion decision, unit (family hypothesis or supported instance), reason, source and horizon before including that unit in an admitted population. A failed candidate may remain in memory without admission. Candidate discovery alone is not that decision.
