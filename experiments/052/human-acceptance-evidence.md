## Overall architecture assessment
The operator reported that architecturally the UI works really well. This is bounded human acceptance evidence for the Control Plane information model, not production-readiness or commercial validation.

## Visual hierarchy defect
The primary UI criticism: different conceptual layers and objects are not visually distinct enough. The interface feels like **“a bunch of very similar boxes.”** This increases orientation burden because hierarchy must be reconstructed intellectually. Treat as an interface/information-hierarchy defect, not a model failure.

## Missing big-picture / Engine-state layer
The dominant missing information layer is the system-level big picture. The operator wants the beginning of AE to communicate conceptually:
- how many opportunities/signals the system has detected;
- rough distribution across funnel/lifecycle states;
- how many require immediate attention.

The underlying need is to understand:
- how the Engine as a whole is performing;
- whether most opportunities are blocked;
- whether a large dormant inventory exists;
- whether dormant opportunities deserve another look;
- where opportunity flow accumulates;
- how efficiently the funnel appears to work.

Do not fabricate funnel counts from the six curated historical fixtures; they are not established as the complete AE inventory.

## Funnel / Engine-state hypothesis
A truthful system-level funnel/distribution view may materially improve orientation, navigation, overall Engine-state comprehension, systemic-bottleneck diagnosis, and dormant/blocked inventory understanding. This is a hypothesis earned for further audit, not validated functionality. Before implementation, determine whether repository evidence can faithfully support funnel semantics and counts.

Trajectory/auditability remains a must-have representation property. The operator's additional exploration included tracing opportunity states. Preserve evidence/history trajectory and drill-down auditability.

## Emerging information-architecture hypothesis
Human use exposed a candidate four-level hierarchy:
1. **ENGINE** — How is AE performing? Funnel / flow / systemic bottlenecks.
2. **CONTROL** — What needs me? Attention / exceptions / decisions / authorization.
3. **POSSIBILITY SPACE** — What opportunities exist and what separates them from value capture? Lifecycle + six-dimensional distance.
4. **EVIDENCE** — Why does AE believe this and how did that belief evolve? Provenance + trajectory + experiments.

This is a conceptual hypothesis exposed by use, not canonical architecture.
