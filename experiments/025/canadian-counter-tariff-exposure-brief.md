## Boundaries that affect the decision

1. **No customs classification is provided.** Every result depends on the supplied Canadian HS tariff item. The commercial description was not used to infer or replace it.
2. **No legal origin determination is guaranteed.** The brief applies the supplied origin facts. The official measure uses eligibility to be marked as a US good under the referenced CUSMA-country marking regulations.
3. **No remission or relief eligibility is guaranteed.** REVIEW REQUIRED means an official route is materially relevant, not that relief will be granted.
4. **This is not a complete landed-cost calculation.** It covers only the bounded incremental September 8 counter-tariff. It excludes ordinary duties, tax, freight, insurance, brokerage, and other trade measures.
5. **A NO is narrow.** It means no exposure was identified under this September 8 measure from the fixed facts; it does not mean the import has no other customs liability.
6. **Freshness matters.** The authoritative sources were checked on 2 September 2026. Tariff schedules, remission orders, and administrative guidance may change, so this brief can become stale.
7. **This is a disposable experimental resolution.** It is designed to support the fixed manifest decision, not to replace a licensed customs broker, legal advice, or an authoritative CBSA determination.
