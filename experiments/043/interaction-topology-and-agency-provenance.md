## 8. Common structural properties

The following are **[OBSERVED]** in both experiments:

- an identifiable fixed actor with direct relation to a live decision;
- a public decision signal and persisted baseline;
- material consequence and unresolved actor-held information;
- a bounded, evidence-linked, falsifiable resolution;
- explicit authorization and a legitimate same-surface intervention path;
- one public intervention, no follow-up, and a predefined 72-hour window;
- an invitation to correct, challenge, or supply a high-information discriminator;
- verified surface publication distinguished from exposure;
- predefined decision-effect criteria and explicit non-commercial scope.

**[DERIVED]** Same-surface placement, direct actor identity, falsifiability, and challenge invitation are common controls and therefore cannot by themselves explain the divergent outcomes.

## 11. Exposure-observability finding

- **[OBSERVED]** 030 proves that same-surface publication can remain exposure-unknown when no actor-specific response/read signal exists.
- **[OBSERVED]** 035's fixed-actor semantic response establishes exposure regardless of which unpreserved notification path led to it.
- **[DERIVED]** Future preflight must specify what would establish exposure and how silence will be classified; “the platform can display a reply” is insufficient.
- **[INFERRED]** Exposure observability is earned as a first-class experimentability condition, not a guarantee that exposure will occur.

## 12. Response/effect-observability finding

- **[OBSERVED]** Both surfaces offered public response channels, but only 035 co-located a response with a public, mutable decision object whose framing could visibly change.
- **[DERIVED]** A response channel is not by itself an effect channel.
- **[INFERRED]** Where the consequential decision is mostly private, the experiment needs an explicit actor report or observable downstream-action path; otherwise even a useful resolution may remain effect-unknown.
- **[UNKNOWN]** The comparison cannot assign causal weight to surface norms, timing, audience, or platform brand.

## 13. Actor-held-information finding

- **[OBSERVED]** 030 asked for an anonymized critical workflow and had unresolved private commercial terms; no response returned either.
- **[OBSERVED]** 035 asked for a concrete dependency or implementation constraint; the actor response supplied implementation facts unavailable to repository reconstruction.
- **[INFERRED]** Actor-facing resolution tests are most discriminating when the resolution exposes one bounded actor-held unknown whose answer can falsify, strengthen, or refine the decision object.
- **[UNKNOWN]** The records do not show whether 030's actor lacked attention, willingness, ability, or need to supply the requested state.

## 14. Challenge/refinement finding

- **[OBSERVED]** Both interventions invited correction without requiring agreement.
- **[OBSERVED]** 035's actor accepted the sequencing direction while correcting the durable abstraction from bare strings to ordered drillable levels and confirming the stop rule was cheap under current implementation.
- **[DERIVED]** The value-bearing evidence was actor-specific refinement, not praise or generic engagement.
- **[UNKNOWN]** 030's lack of response reveals nothing about whether its resolution would have survived challenge.

## 15. Platform-brand inference check

- **[DERIVED]** `n=2` with one case on each platform cannot establish GitHub superiority over Reddit.
- **[INFERRED]** Public decision-object structure and effect observability are more useful bounded descriptors than platform name.
- **[UNKNOWN]** Matched-platform, matched-decision, notification, timing, and actor-propensity effects remain unmeasured.

## 20. Synthetic-evidence-contamination assessment

Potential chain assessed:

```text
AE intervention
→ public platform
→ potentially AI/agent-generated response
→ semantic-looking exchange
→ risk of falsely inferring real economic actor effect
```

- **[UNKNOWN]** 035 cannot rule out AI assistance, delegated generation, automation, or another undisclosed message-origin mechanism.
- **[OBSERVED]** The response was posted under the fixed proposal-author identity, engaged case-specific propositions, supplied implementation-specific state, and altered the public decision record.
- **[INFERRED]** Direct decision authority reduces the risk that the exchange is irrelevant third-party chatter; it does not establish human cognition or executed downstream action.
- **[DERIVED]** Synthetic-evidence contamination was not observed and must not be claimed. The experiment remains exposed to it at the message-origin/human-cognition layer.
- **[DERIVED]** Evidence of a subsequent issue/SIP edit, PR change, implementation/review action, merge, release, adoption, economic outcome, or payment would be needed at progressively later stages. A semantic response alone cannot establish real economic actor effect.

Proposed chain:

```text

→ AGENCY / AUTHORITY

→ ECONOMIC EFFECT

```

with `AUTHORSHIP PROVENANCE` tracked separately.

**EARNED AS PROVISIONAL POLICY.**

- **[DERIVED]** 030 directly demonstrates the break between delivery and exposure.
- **[DERIVED]** 035 demonstrates separable exposure, semantic processing, authoritative identity, and public decision-state refinement.
- **[UNKNOWN]** Neither experiment reaches verified downstream action, economic effect, or value capture.
- **[INFERRED]** `AUTHORSHIP PROVENANCE` belongs in parallel because unaided human origin is not required for every authoritative decision-system effect, while it is decisive for hypotheses specifically about human cognition.
- **[INFERRED]** The chain is an evidence ledger, not a mandatory linear software pipeline or proof that each stage causes the next.

## 23. SAME/ADJACENT/SEPARATE topology assessment

- **[OBSERVED]** Both experiments were `SAME`, yet one remained exposure/effect-unknown and one produced authoritative semantic refinement.
- **[DERIVED]** `SAME` remains useful for describing placement but is insufficient to predict interpretability.
- **[INFERRED]** Supplement `SAME / ADJACENT / SEPARATE` with distinct fields for surface access, actor access, intervention permission, delivery observability, exposure observability, semantic-response observability, decision-state observability, and downstream-action observability.
- **[DERIVED]** The supplement prevents the failure seen in 030 from being mislabeled as value failure and clarifies exactly what 035 did and did not establish.
- **[INFERRED]** This supplement is **EARNED AS PROVISIONAL POLICY**; replacing the existing topology is not earned.

## 24. Minimum future actor-facing preflight standard

The smallest standard supported by 030 × 035 is:

1. **[DERIVED]** Identify a still-changeable consequential decision and a fixed decision-bearing actor or authoritative decision channel.
2. **[DERIVED]** Establish a legitimate, explicitly authorized intervention path and preserve the pre-intervention state.
3. **[DERIVED]** Predeclare what evidence would establish delivery, exposure, semantic engagement, authority, decision-state effect, downstream action, and economic effect; do not let publication stand in for exposure.
4. **[DERIVED]** Require either an observable exposure signal or a response/effect mechanism that itself establishes exposure, with silence preclassified as exposure-unknown when no such signal appears.
5. **[INFERRED]** Anchor the interaction to one bounded actor-held discriminator that can correct, falsify, strengthen, or refine the resolution.
6. **[DERIVED]** If decision effect is the target, require an observable public decision-state change or an explicit bounded actor report; if downstream action/economics is the target, require corresponding later-stage evidence.
7. **[DERIVED]** Record decision authority and authorship provenance separately, using `UNKNOWN` absent direct provenance evidence; verified human authorship is required only for human-cognition hypotheses.
8. **[DERIVED]** Bound interaction count, observation window, silence interpretation, non-response stop, and prohibited outreach before execution.

## 25. Human-versus-decision-system conclusion

- **[INFERRED]** AE's general economic objective is to influence economically consequential decision systems, which may include humans, organizations, or authorized machine/delegated agents.
- **[DERIVED]** A human-cognition experiment requires verified human exposure/comprehension evidence; 035 does not supply it.
- **[DERIVED]** An actor/organizational decision-state experiment may use an authoritative public channel even when message authorship is `UNKNOWN`, provided the claim is limited to the observable decision state.
- **[UNKNOWN]** 030 × 035 do not establish that machine/delegated-agent decisions create economic value.
- **[INFERRED]** Human authority, accountability, consent, authorization, and escalation controls remain necessary regardless of message origin.
