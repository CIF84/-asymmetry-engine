# Spec 031 — RADAR Compounding Test

Run date: 2026-09-02  
Repository baseline: `dc20ffb` on local `main`, fast-forwarded from `origin/main` before research  
Execution mode: research-only; no actors contacted, no public posts, no paid tools, no software built  
Spec 030 isolation: maintained. Its actor, reply state, and artifact were not inspected, modified, contacted, or used as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**B — COMPOUNDING EVIDENCE, NO FORGE HANDOFF.**

The run produced a high-information rejection set rather than a survivor. Learned policy visibly caused early kills at the live-decision, exact-resolution, recoverability, accessibility, and observability gates. It also prevented three superficially attractive cases from receiving product or scalability research. No candidate retains the full combination of a consequential live uncertainty, residual resolution gap, recoverable decisive information, accessible intervention, observable value effect, and acceptable controls.

This is mechanism-level evidence, not a controlled claim that RADAR is a particular percentage faster.

## 2. Primary question tested

Can the current learned research policy generate and discriminate fresh, signal-native opportunity hypotheses cheaply enough to yield either a credible FORGE candidate or a high-information rejection set?

**Answer:** yes, in the second form. Fourteen candidate hypotheses were generated from observable signals across seven surface families. All were killed without construction or outreach, and thirteen were killed before deepening. Three cases received bounded deepening; each converged on a decisive stop observation.

## 7. Deepened candidates

Exactly three candidates were deepened.

### D1 / C01 — GrantScout

- **Strongest support:** the builder states the live stop/go question plainly; current/stale data is genuinely decision-sensitive; one peer builder confirms cross-jurisdiction normalization is hard and valuable.
- **Strongest evidence against:** [the marketplace listing](https://apify.com/beacon_labs/grantscout-mcp) showed 1 total user, 0 monthly active users, no ratings, and at least nine adjacent same-job actors listed on the same page. Generic applicant-type checkers also exist.
- **Exact competition:** Grants.gov/Simpler search and filters; Grant Wire eligibility checker; UseGrants; FindGrants; FedGrantFinder; multiple Apify Grants.gov feeds/scrapers.
- **Decisive uncertainty:** whether any external user repeatedly completes a real grant-search/eligibility workflow through this specific MCP.
- **Cheapest stop observation:** current internal usage. Zero repeat external workflows means do not broaden sources.
- **Cheapest reality-justifying observation:** three unaffiliated users each complete one real search-to-shortlist workflow and identify one decision changed by freshness or eligibility evidence.
- **Disposable resolution:** a one-page stop/go protocol, not another grant tool.
- **Intervention / effect:** Reddit reply could be exposed and answered, but product value would still require actor-reported usage.
- **Control:** CONDITIONAL. Any public reply requires explicit authorization; no pricing/willingness-to-pay test; public data only; do not solicit nonprofits.
- **Decision:** no FORGE handoff now because the target value behavior is not externally observable and the actor already owns the cheapest evidence.

### D2 / C02 — Swan

- **Strongest support:** public production data showed projected pool underfunding, a one-user daily cost spike equal to a month's subscription, and supply concentrated in one or two providers per model.
- **Strongest evidence against:** the author followed with a full position on all six questions, exact peer comparisons, a value-cap priority, an inert model-classification safeguard finding, a minimum provider-count threshold, and a next sequence.
- **Exact competition:** the discussion itself is the exact resolver. OpenRouter's official provider-routing documentation also confirms price/uptime-aware routing and max-price controls.
- **Decisive uncertainty:** providers' actual marginal costs and settlement tolerance, and who bears frontier API invoices.
- **Cheapest stop observation:** read the existing follow-up. It already performs the contemplated decision brief.
- **Cheapest reality-justifying observation:** one full billing period plus provider cost/settlement reports, which the author has already requested.
- **Disposable resolution:** none residual today.
- **Intervention / effect:** SAME GitHub surface; governance changes would be observable.
- **Control:** CONDITIONAL because a public governance intervention needs authorization and provider commercial data must remain voluntary/non-confidential.
- **Decision:** killed at exact-resolution gate; do not duplicate a high-quality existing answer.

### D3 / C03 — Brandfolder

- **Strongest support:** explicit active switching intent, opaque multi-year renewal pressure, forced bundling, unsupported functionality, and a downgrade that no longer meets needs.
- **Strongest evidence against:** G2 and Capterra already list and compare hundreds of DAM products; the decision depends on the customer's private taxonomy, permissions, embedded CDN links, integrations, asset volume, quotes, and migration tests.
- **Exact competition:** [G2 Brandfolder alternatives](https://www.g2.com/products/brandfolder/competitors/alternatives), [G2 DAM category](https://www.g2.com/categories/digital-asset-management), and [Capterra DAM category](https://www.capterra.com/digital-asset-management-software/).
- **Decisive uncertainty:** whether one representative end-to-end asset workflow and metadata/CDN dependency set migrates without material regression.
- **Cheapest stop observation:** verify whether the actor is reachable at a legitimate pre-decision surface. The G2 review is not such a surface.
- **Cheapest reality-justifying observation:** actor voluntarily supplies an anonymized workflow and accepts a parity-test frame.
- **Disposable resolution:** workflow parity checklist, only if the actor independently appears on an accessible decision surface.
- **Intervention / effect:** SEPARATE; no legitimate low-friction path from review evidence to actor decision.
- **Control:** BLOCK for this observed actor under this experiment; no outreach or identity resolution is permitted.
- **Decision:** killed at accessibility before further migration research.

## 21. What did not improve

- This run lacks a predeclared matched historical baseline, so absolute speed improvement cannot be quantified.
- Generic search still returned stale status snapshots; direct surface inspection was necessary to discover closed grant decisions.
- Candidate generation produced many actor-type workflows and few individually observable live buyers.
- Exact-resolution checking was efficient but dominant; future surface allocation should favor signals where the exact answer is less likely to be embedded in the source platform.

## 23. What RADAR learned

Compounding appeared mainly as cheaper kills, not a better survivor. The most valuable sequence was:

1. verify that the decision is genuinely open on the native surface;
2. search for exact functional resolution before category analysis;
3. ask whether the decisive fact is recoverable;
4. test actor and effect observability before designing a resolution;
5. deepen only when the next observation can change advancement.

The run also distinguished three facts that broad discovery often conflates: constructibility (many grant feeds exist), demand (GrantScout showed no active use), and experimental observability (a Reddit response would not prove workflow value).

## 24. What remains unproven

- Whether the learned policy outperforms a fixed baseline under repeated, matched conditions.
- Whether high early-kill rates reflect better policy or a weak evidence environment.
- Whether surface-native discovery can regularly produce accessible individual actors outside forums/GitHub.
- Whether a credible survivor can move from same-surface communication to observable economic behavior cheaply.
- Whether per-candidate time and source telemetry would show sustained improvement across runs.
- Whether any killed candidate would revive under materially new signal, especially actual C01 usage or C02 provider settlement evidence.

## 25. Architecture/research-policy implications

- Add a **native status verification** step before counting a search result as a live decision; search snippets can be stale.
- Keep **exact resolution**, **actor accessibility**, and **effect observability** ahead of detailed opportunity research.
- Record **constructibility, demand evidence, and measurement access separately**; one does not imply the others.
- For product-builder signals, make **repeat external workflow completion** the default value discriminator before broader source coverage or infrastructure.
- Capture **per-candidate active minutes and terminal gate** in the next compounding run, using Markdown only.
- Do not automate the policy yet; one mechanism-level compounding result is insufficient to earn infrastructure.
