## Objective
The long-run objective is a portfolio of recurring economic value with progressively lower mechanical human burden, greater autonomy and reusable learning/infrastructure.

The immediate strategic problem is to identify the **abstract economic mechanism space**, not a named business model.

## Durable constraints
Treat these as objective-function constraints, not candidate hints:
- low ongoing human burden is desirable;
- recurrence matters;
- automation/scalability matter;
- bounded initial manual work is acceptable for learning;
- capital may be used when bounded and justified;
- downside, regulation/control and platform dependency matter;
- existing AE components have no sunk-cost entitlement;
- economic truth ultimately outranks internal elegance.

## Task A — Generate abstract mechanism families
Independently derive mutually distinguishable high-level ways an information/reasoning system could create and capture economic value.

Do not begin from a memorized list of business models.

For each family state:
- what economic event creates the payoff;
- who or what transfers value;
- whether another human/organization must buy from AE;
- whether AE owns delivery/fulfillment;
- what repeats;
- how value is captured;
- principal capital requirement;
- principal operational burden;
- principal dependency;
- principal failure mode;
- plausible path toward low maintenance.

Named real-world implementations may be added only **after** the abstract family is defined, as illustrations rather than taxonomy seeds.

## Task B — Completeness challenge
Try to falsify completeness.

For each family ask:
- what is its conceptual opposite?
- can value be captured without selling anything?
- can value be captured without owning fulfillment?
- can value be captured without an audience?
- can value come from an asset/position rather than a customer payment?
- can AE create value but transfer commercialization to another party?
- can value arise from transaction participation, ownership, licensing, attention, risk-bearing, intermediation, or another mechanism not represented?
- are two apparently different families actually the same economic topology?

Do not force a predetermined number of families.

## Task C — Objective-fit screen
Without external research, qualitatively identify which generated families appear structurally compatible or incompatible with:
- recurring value;
- low operator burden;
- automation;
- bounded initial validation;
- portfolio replication.

This is a structural screen only, not a feasibility ranking.

Do not select a winner.
