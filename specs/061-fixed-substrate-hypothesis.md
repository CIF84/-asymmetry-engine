# SPEC-061 — Fixed Commercialization Substrate Hypothesis

**State:** READY FOR EXECUTION
**Phase:** AE Phase II — Economic Proof

## Decision being tested
Can selecting one genuinely accessible, reusable commercialization environment **before** opportunity discovery shorten AE's evidence-valid path to a first economic event, compared with choosing an opportunity first and then finding its capture route?

**Substrate-first is a hypothesis, not an adopted strategy.** No preferred marketplace, sector, B2B/B2C category, F1–F11 topology or provider is preselected.

## Definitions
A *commercialization substrate* is a concrete operating environment combining (or legitimately linking) discoverable qualified economic intent, permission/access for a new operator, an exposure/acquisition mechanism, an economic exchange/capture route, observable events, and manageable fulfillment/settlement obligations. A marketplace's existence, registered users, payment API, affiliate program, or vendor claim of traffic alone does not qualify.

An *opportunity class* is a materially different recurring paid job/decision within that environment, not a reworded listing.

## Scope and stopping limits
- Public-source, repository-only research; no logins, applications, contact, posts, bids, purchases, enrollments, account creation, live tests, spending, transactions or external publication.

## Sequence

### 1. Select environments from visible economic intent
Find up to three environments that expose concrete current buyer requests, transactions, jobs or other economically actionable demand. Prioritize evidence of *buyer intent*, not aggregate traffic. Capture relevant public primary-source evidence and platform rules.

### 2. Access and economics gate — BEFORE opportunity design
For each environment establish, to the extent possible without an account:
- whether a new AE operator could legitimately enter, and entry prerequisites;
- whether *qualified buyer intent* is observable and reachable by a new entrant, rather than only by established sellers;
- whether offers/bids can be made under actual rules;
- how visibility, competition, acquisition costs and restrictions affect first exposure;
- how orders, fulfillment, disputes, refunds, platform fees and settlement work;
- whether platform lock-in, support and ongoing human work are acceptable;
- whether economic events can be observed distinctly from impressions or unconfirmed contact.

**Stop/defer the environment if any necessary element remains UNKNOWN at the bounded evidence horizon.** Do not infer accessible demand from existing platform revenue.

### 3. Reuse challenge
For a surviving environment, identify **at least two materially distinct plausible opportunity classes** supported by current visible paid demand and compatible with AE's demonstrated information/reasoning capabilities. Explain what distribution, payment, evidence and fulfillment mechanics genuinely reuse; identify class-specific work and shared dependencies.

Do not treat two cosmetic variants as reuse. Do not assert demand for AE merely from existing paid listings.

### 4. Economic treatment
For the strongest qualifying class, design one **minimal truthful economic treatment** with:
- specific buyer/job, bounded offer/deliverable and reason AE could win;
- concrete legitimate exposure route and evidence of plausible qualified reach;
- capture topology chosen by economics, not pre-fixed F1/F4;
- proposed price/fees/full costs, setup and fulfillment hours, risks and support;
- observable qualified exposure, economic event, refund/reversal and settlement states;
- bounded cash/time/downside and stop conditions;
- exact external actions requiring separate human approval.

No proxy-interest staircase. **Do not execute** the treatment.

### 5. Opportunity-first counterfactual
For the **same strongest job**, compare the shortest credible path if AE started from the job and selected any legitimate commercialization route afterward. Account for:
- access and distribution setup;
- transaction/payment setup;
- incremental human effort and time to an economic event;
- settlement and liability;
- reuse for the second opportunity class.

State whether substrate-first yields a **material, evidence-supported advantage**, merely ties, or cannot be distinguished. Do not invent time/CAC savings or assume the counterfactual lacks access.

## Outcomes
- **A — SUBSTRATE-FIRST ADVANTAGE EARNED:** one environment clears access/measurement, has two distinct plausible classes, one launch-ready treatment, and a material supported advantage over opportunity-first.
- **B — NO ADVANTAGE ESTABLISHED:** an environment and treatment may qualify, but fixed-substrate selection is not shown materially better; preserve the treatment as a candidate, not a strategic mandate.
- **N — NONE QUALIFIED:** no environment meets the evidence/access/reuse/treatment gates within three inspections.
- **E — INVALID:** scope, authority or evidence-integrity violation.

Never select A merely because a platform has buyers, a checkout or an API. UNKNOWN is not negative demand evidence. No additional search or follow-on infrastructure is authorized by any outcome.
