## Method

### Stage 1 — Reveal and provenance comparison
Map prior named ideas/recommendations onto F1–F11 only after reading the blind result.
Record:
- independently rediscovered mechanisms;
- mechanisms newly exposed by the blind arm;
- prior strategy-space omissions;
- anchoring implications.

### Stage 2 — Cheap structural pruning
Before web research, test F1–F11 against the Phase II objective:
- first economic proof speed;
- recurrence;
- low mature human burden;
- automation;
- bounded initial validation;
- capital/downside;
- operational complexity;
- platform/concentration dependency;
- factory replication;
- fit with demonstrated AE capabilities.

Prune families only for explicit structural reasons. Preserve a mechanism if a plausible implementation could materially change the result.

Target **3–5 serious survivors**. Do not research all 11 deeply.

### Stage 3 — Translate survivors into plausible implementation classes
Only after pruning, identify concrete 2026 implementations for each survivor. Examples are implementations, not taxonomy.

For each survivor identify at most 2–4 plausible implementation classes that best fit AE's objective/capabilities.

### Stage 4 — Targeted external feasibility research
Fresh public web research is authorized only for serious survivors and their plausible implementations.

Research current evidence on:
- access/control;
- frequency of unresolved economic condition;
- proceeds/full costs;
- decay/exception burden;
- capital exposure;
- genuine reuse/independence;
- acquisition/distribution;
- regulation/control;
- competition/edge durability;
- measurement/attribution;
- maintenance/support;
- automation feasibility.

Prefer primary/authoritative sources for rules, fees, access, APIs, regulation and program terms. Use high-quality secondary/community evidence for practical burden/competition where primary sources cannot answer.

Do not sign up, log in, contact, spend, transact, trade, backtest a live strategy, connect accounts, accept terms, launch or deploy.

### Stage 5 — Adversarial selection
For the leading topology:
- strongest case against it;
- strongest case for nearest rival;
- assumptions driving the ranking;
- evidence that would reverse it;
- whether prior human/model suggestions still appear to influence selection beyond blind rediscovery;
- whether the result survives anonymizing topology labels.

Select one only if evidence genuinely discriminates. Otherwise return B with the smallest empirical head-to-head.

## Special requirement: F5
F5 (retention of gains inside a controlled operation) was independently generated and was not a prior conversational favorite. It must receive explicit structural consideration. Do not promote it merely for novelty; do not prune it without explaining what controlled operation and measurable retained gain would be required.

## Special requirement: F7
If F7 survives structural pruning, assess own-account economic action skeptically:
- plausible edge source;
- recurrence;
- execution access;
- costs/spreads/slippage;
- false-discovery/validation burden;
- capital/drawdown;
- regulatory/control constraints;
- platform/broker dependency;
- monitoring/maintenance;
- automation and failure containment.
No strategy design or live backtest.

## Phase II reset inheritance

- economic outcomes earlier;

- bounded real spending;
- Time to Market Truth;
- recurrence/operator burden;
- meta-work cap;

Exact treatment counts/budgets remain provisional until topology selection.

## Anti-anchoring learning
Evaluate for later protocol adoption:
- human intuition = hypothesis input, not privileged evidence;
- model confidence ≠ independent confirmation;
- taxonomy before tournament;
- independent context before independent generation;
- blind input should minimize navigation;
- independent generation → freeze → reveal → adversarial comparison;
- missing-opposite challenge;
- preferences primarily shape evaluation, not candidate generation.

Do not modify protocol files here.
