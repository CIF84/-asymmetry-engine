# Spec 031 — RADAR Compounding Test

## Status

RADAR research-policy experiment.

Research-only. Do not build software, contact actors, post publicly, run outreach, test pricing, accept payment, or modify Spec 030.

Spec 030 is currently in its observation window. This experiment runs independently in parallel and must not use the CRM case, its actor, or its response state as evidence.

---

## Context

Specs 001–029 accumulated a substantial set of empirical search policies. The project has learned, among other things, that:

- friction is not demand;
- price dispersion is not automatically information asymmetry;
- behavioral evidence can discover a decision question;
- recoverability is a necessary constraint;
- exact existing resolution can kill an otherwise attractive opportunity;
- distribution/accessibility is part of opportunity structure;
- accessible pre-decision surfaces may improve both discovery and intervention experimentability;
- disposable resolution should precede durable product construction;
- the cheapest discriminating observation should usually precede deeper research.

Spec 028 produced one promising survivor by beginning from accessible pre-decision behavior. Specs 029–030 moved that case into FORGE.

The next strategic question is not simply whether RADAR can find another candidate.

It is:

> **Has accumulated learning made RADAR better at discovering economically testable opportunities?**

This is the first explicit test of whether ATLAS is beginning to improve RADAR rather than merely documenting project history.

---

## Primary question

> **Can the current learned research policy generate and discriminate a fresh set of signal-native opportunity hypotheses cheaply enough to produce either one credible FORGE candidate or a high-information rejection set within a bounded research budget?**

The experiment is deliberately domain-agnostic.

Do not optimize for finding a survivor. Optimize for economically relevant truth per unit of research effort.

---

## Compounding hypothesis

The provisional compounding hypothesis is:

```text
PAST EXPERIMENTS
      ↓
LEARNED SEARCH POLICY
      ↓
BETTER NEXT OBSERVATIONS
      ↓
FASTER / CHEAPER DISCRIMINATION
      ↓
BETTER SURVIVORS OR CHEAPER KILLS
      ↓
MORE LEARNING
```

Evidence of compounding may therefore appear as either:

- a credible survivor reached with less wasted research;
- faster rejection of weak candidates;
- fewer repeated historical mistakes;
- better identification of the decisive uncertainty;
- better candidate quality before deep research;
- more evidence generated per unit of active time;
- clearer recognition of when no candidate deserves advancement.

A survivor is **not required** for evidence of process improvement.

## Discovery origin

Begin from **observable pre-commitment economic decision signals**.

The target topology is provisionally:

```text
OBSERVABLE SIGNAL
      ↓
ACTOR / ACTOR TYPE
      ↓
LIVE DECISION
      ↓
ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE
      ↓
MEANINGFUL UNCERTAINTY
      ↓
RECOVERABLE INFORMATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RESOLUTION GAP
      ↓
ACCESSIBLE DECISION SURFACE
      ↓
OBSERVABLE EFFECT
      ↓
CHEAP EXPERIMENT POSSIBILITY
```

This topology is a research prior, not a rigid ontology.

If reality exposes a materially different but stronger topology, record it rather than forcing the evidence into this structure.

---

## Domain neutrality

Do not begin from a preferred industry, technology, product idea, or business model.

In particular, do not preferentially search for:

- CRM decisions;
- SaaS migrations;
- B2B software;
- Reddit opportunities;
- tariff/compliance calculators;
- repair-versus-replace tools;
- EV configuration tools;
- any other previously tested project domain.

A previously encountered domain may appear only when a genuinely fresh signal independently surfaces a different decision structure.

Candidate generation must remain signal-native.

## Surface diversity

Inspect at least **4 materially different decision-signal surface families**.

At least **2 must not be Reddit, generic forums, or generic web search**.

Possible families include, without limitation:

- public Q&A / forums;
- search/query evidence;
- comparison or configuration surfaces;
- marketplaces and listings;
- public procurement/request feeds;
- public eligibility/application workflows;
- regulatory or rule-change surfaces;
- product/service review ecosystems where a decision remains open;
- GitHub issues/discussions exposing economic implementation decisions;
- public professional communities;
- booking/selection workflows;
- public calculators/checkers with unresolved adjacent decisions;
- other surfaces discovered during research.

Do not choose surfaces merely to satisfy diversity. Record unproductive families honestly.

---

## Signal-before-hypothesis rule

For every counted candidate preserve:

```text
RAW SIGNAL
→ OBSERVED BEHAVIOR / STATE
→ ACTOR
→ LIVE DECISION
→ UNCERTAINTY
→ ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE
→ POSSIBLE RESOLUTION
```

If the business idea clearly existed before the observed signal, do not count it.

Do not search backward for evidence supporting an idea generated from memory.

---

## Candidate target

Generate **10–15 signal-native candidate hypotheses**.

Do not manufacture weak candidates solely to reach 10. If the evidence environment produces fewer credible signals within the time limit, stop and report that as evidence.

Each counted candidate must minimally identify:

- raw signal and surface;
- actor or actor type;
- decision still open;
- economic consequence;
- decision-relevant uncertainty;
- plausible recoverable information;
- possible bounded resolution;
- likely intervention surface;
- observable effect if resolution were useful.

---

## Learned kill sequence

Apply the cheapest relevant gates sequentially.

Default order:

```text
1. LIVE PRE-COMMITMENT DECISION?
2. ECONOMICALLY CONSEQUENTIAL?
3. MEANINGFUL DECISION UNCERTAINTY?
4. DECISIVE INFORMATION RECOVERABLE?
5. ADEQUATE EXACT RESOLUTION ALREADY EXISTS?
6. RESOLUTION CONSTRUCTIBLE AT LOW COST?
7. DECISION SURFACE ACCESSIBLE?
8. EFFECT OF RESOLUTION OBSERVABLE?
9. REGULATORY / PLATFORM / DATA CONTROLS FEASIBLE?
10. CHEAP REAL EXPERIMENT PLAUSIBLE?
```

This is a default research policy, not a requirement to perform ten equally deep investigations.

Kill immediately when a cheap upstream observation makes later work economically irrational.

Do not gather evidence that cannot change the advancement decision.

---

## Exact-resolution gate

For candidates surviving the cheap upstream gates, search explicitly for functional existing resolution using:

```text
ACTOR × DECISION × INPUTS × RESOLUTION × OUTPUT × TIMING
```

Search for the bounded job, not merely category competitors.

If an adequate accessible resolver already performs the job at the relevant decision moment, kill the candidate unless a specific residual resolution gap remains.

Do not repair failed research tooling when already-observed evidence is sufficient to make the decision.

---

## Recoverability

Classify decisive information as HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW recoverability.

### HIGH

The necessary facts are public, actor-supplied, derivable, or available from stable authoritative evidence.

### MEDIUM

Most facts are recoverable and the remaining uncertainty can be bounded by one or a few explicit actor-supplied variables or tests.

### LOW

The decisive information depends mainly on inaccessible private institutional state, confidential records, case-specific professional judgment, or evidence available only after the decision.

LOW normally kills the candidate unless a different resolution can avoid the hidden variable.

---

## Accessible decision surface

For serious candidates ask:

> **Could a disposable resolution be placed into a real decision context cheaply enough to observe its effect without bespoke relationship-building for every exposure?**

Classify discovery/intervention relationship:

- **SAME** — same surface can plausibly support both;
- **ADJACENT** — a low-friction transition is required;
- **SEPARATE** — materially different acquisition/distribution path required.

SAME is not automatically superior if the surface creates platform, trust, legal, or scale constraints.

---

## Control feasibility

Before recommending a real experiment, perform a lightweight manual control check across only relevant dimensions:

- evidence/provenance;
- source/data legitimacy;
- privacy;
- platform rules;
- communication/outreach constraints;
- regulatory/legal sensitivity;
- commercial-activity implications where relevant;
- authorization required for consequential external action;
- resource/time/spend exposure.

Classify candidate experimentability as:

- PASS;
- CONDITIONAL;
- REVIEW REQUIRED;
- BLOCK.

Unknown control state must never silently become PASS.

Do not build a regulatory rules engine or perform exhaustive legal research during this discovery experiment.

---

## Deepening limit

Deepen at most **3 candidates**.

Deepening is justified only when the candidate survives enough cheap gates that the next observation has higher expected information value than continuing broad discovery.

For each deepened candidate identify:

- strongest evidence supporting the opportunity;
- strongest evidence against it;
- exact functional competition;
- decisive remaining uncertainty;
- cheapest discriminator;
- plausible disposable resolution;
- accessible intervention mechanism;
- observable behavioral/economic effect;
- control status;
- why the next experiment is or is not justified.

Do not design a durable product.

---

## At most one survivor

Recommend at most **one** candidate for FORGE or one bounded discriminator.

If several candidates appear promising, select the one whose **next uncertainty can be reduced most cheaply and decisively**, not the one with the most exciting narrative.

Record runners-up without advancing them.

---

## Research-policy challenge

For every deepened candidate ask explicitly:

> **What is the cheapest observation that could prove we should stop?**

Also ask:

> **If this candidate survives, what is the cheapest observation that could justify interacting with reality rather than doing more research?**

This protects against both false-positive enthusiasm and an over-defensive Engine that researches forever.

---

## Compounding measurement

The experiment must evaluate not only candidate outcomes but the quality of the research process.

Record whether prior learning visibly prevented any of these historical failure modes:

- mistaking friction for demand;
- mistaking price dispersion for asymmetry;
- absorbing adjacent intent into a weak hypothesis;
- researching scalability before value;
- overlooking an exact existing resolver;
- discovering distribution only after resolution construction;
- treating an inaccessible actor as experimentally reachable;
- interpreting inability to observe behavior as negative demand evidence;
- building durable infrastructure before the hypothesis earns it;
- continuing research after a decisive kill observation.

For each prevented recurrence, cite the candidate or research step where the learned policy changed behavior.

Do not claim compounding merely because the framework was remembered.

---

## Compounding verdict dimensions

Assess these qualitatively at the end:

### C1 — Search efficiency

Did learned policy reduce wasted research or produce earlier kills?

### C2 — Candidate quality

Did the generated set contain more decision-specific and experimentally tractable hypotheses than earlier broad discovery runs?

### C3 — Error non-recurrence

Did the process avoid previously observed failure modes because of learned policy?

### C4 — Discriminator quality

Did serious candidates converge toward smaller, cheaper, more decisive uncertainties?

### C5 — Experimentability

Did the search produce candidates whose real-world value could be tested cheaply and legitimately?

### C6 — Economic evidence efficiency

Did the run generate useful economic learning within the time/cost envelope, regardless of survivor count?

Classify each:

- SUPPORTED;
- WEAKLY SUPPORTED;
- CONTRADICTED;
- NOT TESTED.

Do not combine these into a numeric score.

## Time and cost envelope

Target active research time: **60–90 minutes**.

Hard active-time ceiling: **90 minutes**.

Preferred incremental spend: **€0**.

Maximum incremental paid research spend: **€2**, and only when one bounded paid observation has clearly higher expected information value than available open evidence.

Do not exceed the time ceiling to rescue a weak candidate or achieve the candidate quota.

---

## Verdicts

### A — COMPOUNDING EVIDENCE + FORGE CANDIDATE

At least one candidate survives the current gates with concrete evidence of consequential live uncertainty, recoverability, residual resolution gap, resolution feasibility, accessible intervention, observable effect, and acceptable control feasibility; and the run provides meaningful evidence that accumulated research policy improved discovery/discrimination economics.

Recommend exactly one disposable FORGE experiment.

### B — COMPOUNDING EVIDENCE, NO FORGE HANDOFF

No candidate yet justifies FORGE, but the run demonstrates meaningful improvement in search efficiency, error non-recurrence, discriminator quality, or economic evidence efficiency.

Recommend exactly one research-policy or bounded-candidate discriminator next action.

### C — NO CLEAR COMPOUNDING EVIDENCE

The run is valid but does not demonstrate that accumulated learning materially improved RADAR economics.

State whether the problem appears to be candidate generation, search policy, evidence access, comparison baseline, or another cause.

Do not rescue the result by redefining compounding after observing it.

### D — INVALID COMPOUNDING TEST

The experiment cannot answer the primary question because execution materially violated the design, evidence was inadequate, time/cost telemetry was unusable, candidate generation was non-signal-native, or another failure prevents interpretation.

---

## Interpretation caution

This is an early compounding test, not a controlled benchmark.

Earlier experiments differed in domains, tools, candidate counts, evidence availability, and objectives. Therefore do not make unsupported claims such as "RADAR is 37% faster."

The strongest legitimate evidence will be mechanism-level:

- a learned gate visibly prevents repeated waste;
- decisive uncertainties are identified earlier;
- exact-resolution competition is checked before costly deepening;
- inaccessible intervention topology is recognized before FORGE;
- fewer weak candidates receive expensive research;
- a credible survivor reaches experiment design with fewer unnecessary steps.

Quantitative performance comparisons require future repeated telemetry.

## Governing principles

> **Optimize for time to economic evidence.**

> **The cheapest decisive observation should precede deeper research.**

> **Behavioral evidence discovers the question. Authoritative evidence should answer it.**

> **Exact existing resolution can kill an otherwise elegant opportunity.**

> **Distribution and intervention accessibility belong inside opportunity structure.**

> **An experiment unable to observe its target behavior is invalid, not negative.**

> **Disposable before durable.**

> **As evidence survives cheap gates, the burden should shift toward running the cheapest real experiment.**

> **Compounding exists only when past learning improves future economic search, not when the project merely accumulates documentation.**

> **Do not automate weak ideas. Automate repeated, empirically earned reasoning only when doing so improves future experiment economics.**
