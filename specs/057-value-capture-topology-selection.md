# Spec 057 — Value-Capture Topology Selection

## Status
READY FOR EXECUTION

## Type
Strategic topology-completion and fresh external-feasibility research experiment. No economic action, trading, spending, product launch, affiliate enrollment, account connection, actor contact, deployment, or production implementation.

## Baseline
Execute from synchronized canonical main at or after Experiment 056 published closure `87d5ea2`.

## Why
Experiment 056 earned a directional Phase II economic reset but failed to justify its affiliate-first substrate recommendation. Independent blind review found the strategy tournament incomplete because it omitted direct exploitation and mixed search space, capture topology and experimental regime.

The next decision is therefore upstream of any Phase II launch:

> Which value-capture topology gives AE the strongest credible route to recurring, low-maintenance captured value?

This experiment must complete that strategy space independently, then use fresh external evidence to discriminate feasibility.

## Anti-anchoring control
Human/model suggestions are hypothesis inputs, not privileged evidence.

Named examples previously discussed — including affiliate/referral, trading, arbitrage, paid information, SaaS and others — must not determine the initial candidate taxonomy.

Use this sequence:

1. OBJECTIVE + CONSTRAINTS
2. BLIND TOPOLOGY GENERATION
3. COMPLETENESS / MISSING-OPPOSITE CHALLENGE
4. FREEZE CANDIDATE SPACE
5. ONLY THEN reveal/consult prior named examples and 056 blind/review artifacts
6. FRESH EXTERNAL FEASIBILITY RESEARCH
7. ADVERSARIAL COMPARISON
8. DECISION OR EXPLICIT UNRESOLVED RESULT

The artifact must timestamp or clearly mark the blind-space freeze before reading 056's named topology conclusions/review. If the executor cannot enforce this ordering, stop INVALID rather than pretend independence.

## Objective
Select, or narrow to the smallest unresolved set of, value-capture topologies most likely to help AE reach:
- valid economic exchange/capture quickly;
- recurrence;
- low ongoing human burden;
- scalable/automatable mechanics;
- acceptable downside/control/regulatory exposure;
- reusable factory learning.

Long-term low-maintenance/passive-ish income is the objective; first money is an intermediate evidence transition.

## Constraints / operator utility
Use only durable project constraints and approved objective:
- autonomy and low ongoing human burden matter;
- avoid business models structurally dependent on heavy bespoke support/networking if equivalent alternatives exist;
- modest manual work is acceptable for initial validation;
- execution/build cost may be low enough that market/economic action can outperform proxy validation;
- capital can be spent when bounded and evidence-producing;
- no existing AE component is protected by sunk cost.

Do not use the user's named substrate preferences during blind generation.

## Phase A — Blind topology generation
Using objective + pre-056 durable AE capabilities/constraints, independently derive the high-level ways AE could capture value.

Generate topology families, not brand/business-model examples.

For each topology define:
- who/what supplies the economic payoff;
- whether a customer/buyer is required;
- whether AE sells information, mediates a transaction, acts itself, owns a product, monetizes attention, licenses an asset, or another independently derived mechanism;
- value creation path;
- value capture path;
- recurrence mechanism;
- principal operational burden;
- principal failure mode.

## Completeness challenge
Before freeze, test:
- direct vs mediated capture;
- customer vs no-customer;
- own action vs advise/refer another actor;
- own fulfillment vs outsourced fulfillment;
- transaction-margin vs fee/commission vs asset return vs attention;
- novel opportunity vs proven-market adaptation;
- active service vs durable asset;
- centralized platform dependency vs diversified access;
- high-frequency repeated events vs low-frequency high-value events.

Use the “missing opposite” test for every favored topology.

Freeze the topology space before consulting prior 056 topology recommendations.

## Phase B — Reveal / comparison
After freeze, inspect:
- Experiment 056 blind strategy-space challenge;
- Experiment 056 review/debrief;
- prior named human/model examples.

Record:
- independently rediscovered topologies;
- topologies added only after reveal;
- blind candidates missing from 056;
- whether prior suggestions materially anchored or distorted the space.

## Phase C — Fresh external feasibility research
External web research is authorized for public feasibility evidence only.

Research current 2026 conditions sufficient to compare serious topology survivors.

Prefer primary/authoritative sources for:
- platform/program terms;
- fees/commissions;
- broker/market/API rules;
- regulatory obligations;
- advertising/affiliate disclosure requirements;
- payment/platform economics;
- market-access requirements.

Use high-quality secondary/community evidence for:
- acquisition difficulty;
- operator burden;
- maintenance;
- competitive intensity;
- practical failure modes;
- realistic implementation experience.

Do not sign up, log in, contact, transact, trade, spend, scrape prohibited sources, or accept terms.

## Serious-survivor feasibility dimensions
For every serious topology compare:

1. Time to first valid market/economic truth.
2. Economic observability.
3. Recurrence/frequency.
4. Path to low maintenance.
5. Automation potential.
6. Capital required.
7. Capital at risk / downside.
8. Customer acquisition requirement.
9. Distribution/platform dependency.
10. Fulfillment burden.
11. Support burden.
12. Regulatory/control distance.
13. Competitive intensity.
14. Edge durability / commoditization risk.
15. Attribution/measurement quality.
16. Unit-economics plausibility.
17. Reusable factory learning.
18. Fit with demonstrated AE capabilities.
19. New capabilities required.
20. Information value on failure.
21. Reversibility.
22. Scalability.
23. Time/effort to replicate a second asset/mechanism.

No fake composite score. Qualitative ranks and explicit assumptions are preferred.

## Direct-exploitation special challenge
Direct exploitation must receive a serious but skeptical analysis.

For trading/arbitrage-like implementations research:
- what plausible informational/structural edge classes remain accessible to a small independent actor;
- frequency and decay;
- data/API access;
- transaction costs, fees, spreads/slippage;
- statistical validation / backtest false-discovery risk;
- capital and drawdown;
- leverage risk;
- market/broker/platform dependency;
- applicable regulatory/control constraints for own-account activity versus managing/soliciting others;
- monitoring burden;
- automation/operational risk;
- tax/accounting complexity at a high level;
- whether the topology plausibly supports the low-maintenance objective.

Do not design or recommend a live trading strategy, select securities, place trades, or connect accounts.

## Third-party transaction special challenge
For affiliate/referral/lead-like topology research:
- program access/approval;
- commission/lead economics;
- attribution/cookie/tracking constraints;
- traffic acquisition;
- SEO/platform dependency;
- disclosure/privacy requirements;
- merchant concentration;
- reversals/settlement;
- content freshness;
- support/fulfillment boundaries;
- whether reusable decision-resolution machinery can transfer across niches.

Do not enroll in programs.

## Direct-paid resolution special challenge
For paid information/tool/service topology research:
- payment friction;
- buyer trust/acquisition;
- pricing observability;
- refund/support expectations;
- liability/advice boundaries;
- delivery standardization;
- recurrence;
- productization path;
- whether manual validation can transition to low-maintenance assets.

## Proven-market adaptation
For each serious topology ask whether starting from demonstrated existing transactions materially reduces demand uncertainty.

Distinguish:
- evidence that a market exists;
- evidence that AE has an accessible advantage within it.

Do not treat competitor revenue as proof AE can acquire customers or edge.

## AI-execution-cost crossover
Assess where 2026 AI-assisted implementation changes the optimal validation regime.

Ask:
> When is the all-in cost of another proxy experiment greater than the all-in cost/risk of a truthful economically revealing treatment?

Include acquisition, capital risk, fulfillment, support, controls and waiting — not coding alone.

## Money-machine fit
For each survivor distinguish:
- first economic proof;
- repeatable economic mechanism;
- low-maintenance asset;
- portfolio/factory replication.

A topology that produces fast money but structurally requires high human labor may be a validation substrate but not the terminal Phase II factory.

## Decision standard
A preferred topology may be selected only if:
- it survives blind generation and completeness challenge;
- fresh evidence supports feasibility;
- its fatal unknowns are testable within a bounded Phase II entry experiment;
- it has a plausible recurrence/low-maintenance path;
- no omitted topology plausibly dominates under the same objective.

If evidence cannot discriminate, return B and specify the smallest empirical head-to-head needed.

## Anti-confirmation challenge
Before finalizing:
- construct the strongest case against the leading topology;
- construct the strongest case for its nearest rival;
- state what evidence would reverse the ranking;
- ask whether the winner depends on assumptions imported from the user's examples;
- ask whether the same result would likely emerge if topology names were anonymized.
