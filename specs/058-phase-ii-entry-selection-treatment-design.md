# Spec 058 — Phase II Entry: One Decision Job to Economic Treatment

## Status
READY FOR EXECUTION

## Phase
**AE Phase II — Economic Proof**

## Purpose
Find **at most one** fresh decision job that can support a truthful, comparable F1/F4 economic test, apply only cheap fatal gates, and—if one qualifies—produce a launch-ready paired treatment design for explicit human authorization.

This experiment is the bridge from strategic review to economic reality.

It is not another generic demand-validation experiment and not permission to launch.

## Strategic baseline
Experiments 056–057B collectively establish:
- AE's strategic review is complete enough to begin Phase II.
- Economic outcomes now outrank further generic research/meta-work.
- Cheap fatal gates should precede economically revealing action.
- F1 direct compensated provision and F4 exchange-contingent intermediation are the smallest currently earned capture-topology comparison.
- B2B software choice is illustrative only and **not preselected**.
- F2/F5/F7 remain conditional reserves; F10 is a soft deferred family.
- First economic proof, recurrence, low-maintenance operation and portfolio replication are separate milestones.
- Qualified exposure is required before interpreting negative market evidence.
- No Phase II budget or launch has yet been approved.

## Primary question
> Can AE identify one real decision job where the same useful bounded resolution can legitimately reach a comparable qualified audience under both F1 and F4, such that the next step can be real economic exposure rather than another proxy experiment?

## Required outcome
Exactly one of:

### Q — QUALIFY
One job passes all fatal gates. Produce a launch-ready paired F1/F4 treatment specification and stop for human authorization.

### N — NONE
No candidate qualifies within the bounded search. Return no treatment. Do not lower gates, extend search, or substitute a merely interesting opportunity.

### E — INVALID
Method/scope/integrity failure.

There is no “promising but keep researching” outcome.

## Search principle
Search **fresh** commercially active decision spaces. Do not begin from historical AE opportunities or the illustrative software-choice example.

Prefer environments where:
- economic transactions already occur;
- a real decision precedes the transaction;
- decision quality plausibly matters economically;
- public/licensed information can materially improve the decision;
- the decision recurs across independent buyers/decision owners;
- qualified audiences are legitimately reachable;
- F1 and F4 can plausibly operate on the same job/audience;
- treatment can be delivered without building durable software;
- economic events can be observed.

Existing transaction flow is a prior for market existence, not proof of AE advantage.

## Search breadth / velocity
This is a **bounded selection sprint**, not comprehensive opportunity discovery.

- Generate/search enough candidates to find one qualifier, but inspect **no more than 12 candidate jobs**.
- Use broad public web research and structured local/business/product search where useful.
- Stop immediately when one candidate clearly qualifies.
- If none qualifies after 12, return N.
- Do not create a ranked backlog of near-misses for continuation.
- Record rejected candidates and fatal reason briefly for learning.

Target active time: 45–90 minutes. Hard ceiling: 120 minutes. External spend: €0.

## Candidate unit
A candidate is a **decision job**, not an industry, product, keyword or business model.

Represent it as:

> **[specific decision owner] choosing [specific thing/action] within [decision horizon/context] because [economic consequence].**

Bad:
- “cybersecurity”
- “travel”
- “software”
- “affiliate niche”

Better:
- a bounded recurring decision with identifiable transaction context and observable resolution inputs.

Do not reuse these examples as candidate hints.

## Cheap fatal gates
A candidate must pass all gates before treatment design.

### G1 — Real economic transaction
Evidence that money already changes hands around the underlying decision.

### G2 — Specific recurring decision job
The decision recurs across independent decision owners often enough to support repeated exposure. Do not require proof of AE demand.

### G3 — Residual resolution gap
Existing free/cheap exact solutions do not already make AE's proposed bounded resolution redundant.

Perform only a cheap exact-alternative challenge. Do not write a market landscape.

### G4 — Recoverable resolution
AE can truthfully produce a useful bounded resolution from legitimate public/licensed information without privileged actor-held data or speculative claims.

### G5 — F1 path
A plausible decision owner could directly pay AE for the bounded contribution. Payment infrastructure existence alone is insufficient; the offer must be coherent and fulfillable.

### G6 — F4 path
A legitimate third-party transaction-contingent compensation route plausibly exists for the same underlying decision. Verify current public program/partner terms sufficiently to establish plausibility. Do not enroll.

### G7 — Comparable qualified audience
There is at least one legitimate acquisition/exposure surface that can plausibly reach comparable qualified decision owners for both arms.

If F1 would depend on marketplace buyer traffic while F4 depends on unrelated SEO traffic, the topology comparison is confounded. A package-level comparison is allowed only if explicitly justified as the more economically relevant question; default is common/comparable exposure.

### G8 — Measurable economic events
The treatment can prospectively distinguish:
- qualified exposure;
- F1 order/commitment;
- F1 fulfillment/refund/retained receipt;
- F4 attributed event;
- F4 payable/locked/reversed state where available;
- F4 settlement;
- human delivery/exception time;
- direct cash costs.

### G9 — Bounded treatment
No durable product/platform/content factory is required. The first treatment can be built/delivered manually or with disposable tooling within a small bounded effort.

### G10 — Regulatory/control viability
No obvious legal, regulatory, platform, contractual, privacy, IP/data-rights or authority blocker makes the proposed test illegitimate or disproportionate.

This is a bounded feasibility check, not legal advice.

### G11 — Economic relevance
The underlying transaction/value and plausible capture mechanism are large enough that a successful event could be meaningful after payment/referral fees and bounded human effort. Do not require proven profitability.

### G12 — Honest falsifiability
A negative result can be interpreted only if qualified exposure and event capture are valid. The design must be capable of distinguishing “no economic response” from “nobody meaningfully saw/could complete the treatment.”

## No proxy staircase
After G1–G12 pass, do **not** insert separate experiments for:
- stated interest;
- surveys;
- actor interviews;
- generic engagement;
- “would you pay?”;
- public posting without exposure telemetry;
- non-economic decision-state effects.

If a gate can only be resolved by such a proxy chain, reject the candidate for this Phase II entry sprint unless the proxy is legally/control-required and materially cheaper than treatment.

## F1 treatment design
For a qualifying job specify:
- decision owner;
- bounded deliverable;
- truthful value proposition;
- fixed scope;
- price hypothesis and rationale;
- payment route;
- fulfillment method;
- delivery time/capacity;
- refund/cancellation boundary;
- what is explicitly not promised;
- qualified exposure definition;
- economic event ledger;
- full-cost fields;
- stop conditions.

No SaaS/product build unless unavoidable; if unavoidable, candidate fails G9.

## F4 treatment design
For the same qualifying job specify:
- useful decision aid/resolution;
- merchant/provider/program route;
- current public eligibility/commission/event terms;
- disclosure requirement;
- attribution mechanism;
- prohibited traffic/behavior;
- settlement/locking/reversal timing;
- what event counts as early economic evidence versus settled capture;
- qualified exposure definition;
- economic event ledger;
- maintenance burden;
- stop conditions.

No enrollment or acceptance of terms in this experiment.

## Common treatment design
Hold constant where reasonably possible:
- decision job;
- resolution logic/source standard;
- decision horizon;
- qualification criteria;
- acquisition surface;
- observation horizon;
- evidence ledger.

Use non-overlapping cohorts. Randomization may be proposed only if practical/legal and not required for launch.

Do not claim pure causal topology isolation when intrinsic offer/payment differences remain. The practical question is which economic package produces stronger evidence and economics under a comparable job.

## Price / commission / sample parameters
Use current external evidence to propose—not activate:
- F1 price;
- F4 expected event value/range where public;
- maximum direct cash spend;
- maximum human delivery/setup time;
- minimum economically interpretable qualified-exposure denominator;
- observation horizon;
- settlement horizon.

Do not use arbitrary round numbers without rationale. Preserve uncertainty.

The treatment may be launch-ready even if settlement occurs after the first observation window, provided payable/locked/settled states are separately tracked.

## Search-source policy
External public research is authorized.

Prefer:
- primary merchant/program/payment/platform/regulatory sources;
- current public prices/terms;
- search/community evidence only for discovery or practical burden where authoritative sources cannot answer.

Do not:
- log in;
- enroll;
- contact;
- accept terms;
- spend;
- launch;
- publish;
- transact;
- create accounts;
- scrape prohibited sources.

## Candidate-memory capture
Use the 054 identity/evidence semantics prospectively in the artifact:
- candidate identity;
- family/decision-instance distinction where relevant;
- admission state;
- evidence horizon;
- blockers;
- provenance;
- UNKNOWN preservation.

Do not build persistence/software. The artifact is the prospective record for this sprint.

## F2/F5/F7/F10 interruption rule
Do not broaden the search into these families.

However, if fresh evidence encountered **incidentally** during the sprint satisfies a previously recorded reopening condition strongly enough that one of F2/F5/F7/F10 clearly dominates the current F1/F4 candidate, record it as a strategic interruption and stop Q/N selection for human review.

Do not research these families proactively.

## Meta-work rule
No UI, architecture, ontology, scheduler, automation, dashboard, generic telemetry, content engine or production infrastructure.

If the experiment cannot proceed without such work, return N or E rather than building it.

## Human role
Human input is required only for:
- consequential authorization after Q;
- cash/time downside approval;
- commercial/legal obligation acceptance;
- any treatment detail that genuinely depends on operator preference/risk tolerance.

Do not ask the human to perform mechanical research, transcription or bookkeeping.

## Launch authorization boundary
**Q does not authorize launch.**

After Q, stop with a concrete authorization packet stating exactly:
- what will be published/contacted/enrolled/paid;
- where;
- under which terms;
- maximum cash at risk;
- maximum human time;
- treatment duration;
- stop conditions;
- data/evidence captured;
- reversibility/obligations.

Human approval is required before any external treatment action.

## Success standard for Experiment 058
Success is **not revenue**.

Success is either:
- one legitimately qualifying job with a launch-ready economically revealing treatment; or
- a truthful bounded N showing that the current search did not find one without lowering standards.

The following experiment, if Q is authorized, should produce real market/economic exposure.
